## 摘要

We study stochastic bandits with reward-dependent delays, possibly infinite, whose reports omit the originating round. For each $c\in[0,1)$, Balanced Historical Optimism (BHO) has a delay term controlled by the largest suboptimal-arm quantile at level $1-cΔ_i$, without requiring optimal-arm reports....
